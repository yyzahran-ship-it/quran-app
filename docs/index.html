Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Data we collect

We collect no data. No analytics, no accounts, no tracking, no ads — ever.

There are no analytics SDKs, crash-reporting tools, advertising networks, or tracking pixels in this app.

Stored on your device only

The following is created by you and lives only on your device. We cannot access it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

  • Bookmarks — verses you have saved
  • Notes — text notes on verses
  • Hifz cards — your memorisation progress
  • Preferences — theme, font size, chosen reciter

Internet access

The app uses the internet only to stream Quran audio recitation. All Quran text and translations are bundled in the app and work fully offline.

Audio is streamed from everyayah.com. Their privacy policy governs those requests.

Third-party services

Service Purpose Data sent
everyayah.com Audio streaming Your IP address (standard HTTP request); no personal data

Children

This app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. It contains no advertising and requires no user account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
